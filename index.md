# About Me

My name is **Guoxin Shi (石国昕)**. I am a PhD student at **KTH Royal Institute of Technology**, working in the [Mobility Informatics Lab](https://zhenliangma.com/).

My research focuses on **decision-making under deep uncertainty**: how intelligent agents can make robust decisions when models, information, and future conditions are fundamentally uncertain. I am particularly interested in **agentic LLMs** as a new paradigm for decision-making under such conditions, with **transport planning and mobility systems** as a primary application domain.

Before starting my PhD, I spent three years in industry working on real-world decision-making systems. One lesson I took from that experience is how difficult it is to build models that remain reliable outside the conditions in which they were developed. Practical systems often depend heavily on local and context-specific adjustments. This motivates a broader question behind my research: **how can intelligent systems make robust decisions when the future is deeply uncertain?**

Beyond research, I enjoy creative works, especially films, comics, and music, and I may occasionally write about them here :)
