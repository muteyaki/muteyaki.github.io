# About Me

My name is **Guoxin Shi (石国昕)**. I am a PhD student at **KTH Royal Institute of Technology**, working in the [Mobility Informatics Lab](https://zhenliangma.com/).

My research focuses on **decision-making under deep uncertainty**, with particular interests in **agentic LLMs** and their applications to **transport planning and mobility systems**.

Before starting my PhD, I received my master's degree from Tsinghua University and my bachelor's degree from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China. I also spent three years at Meituan, working on real-world decision-making systems.

Beyond research, I enjoy films, comics, and music, and I may occasionally write about them here :)
